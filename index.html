01 / Overview

Welcome to the Young Quantum Researchers Summer School!

Get ready to learn, collaborate and connect with fellow young researchers.

Join us at the University of Edinburgh for a three-day introduction to quantum computing and careers in research. The school takes place at the Edinburgh Futures Institute and is designed for students with an EH postcode who are leaving S5 or S6.

You’ll hear from Quantum Software Lab researchers in lectures, write code alongside other students and ask researchers about their work in Q&A sessions.

02 / About

A first look at quantum computing.

What can a quantum computer do, and how do you write a program for one?

Explore these questions in Edinburgh with researchers from the Quantum Software Lab. Try quantum algorithms, write code with other students and ask the people who work in the field how they got started.

06 / Eligibility

Who can apply?

We’d love to hear from you if you meet all four of the following criteria.

  • You attend a school in the Edinburgh area.
  • You’re finishing S5 or S6 this school year.
  • You’ve taken or are taking Higher courses in at least two of Maths, Physics and Computing Science.
  • You didn’t attend the 2026 YQR summer school.

Some coding experience is preferred, particularly in Python. Beginners are welcome to apply.
